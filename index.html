About Me

Welcome to my portfolio.
I am a master's student in Geography and Teaching Assistant at the University of Colorado Boulder, focusing on GIS, remote sensing, LMs, and conflict studies. I'm interested in how technology plays a role in contemporary conflict (insurgency, interstate, tribal, etc.) and how it can be used to better understand modern conflict, humanitarian risks, and government accountability. I received my BA in International Affairs in 2026 with a focus in Foreign Policy & Political Geography, and a foundation in GIS & Data Science. I graduated with latin honors (summa) and completed a thesis on AI & Drone use in contemporary kill chains (yeah, this research still haunts me). I'm pursuing geography because there's something special GIS/space and its ability to probe the world(s) around us...
Not all I do is academic fortunately! I also have many hobbies: Cycle/Downhill/XC MTB, Ice/Rock/Mtn Climbing (I guide too), Ski Alpine/Tour, hike, back/fastpack, and pretty much most things outdoors (except fishing; too low cortisol). Currently trying to get back into tri and eventually would like to hike-n-fly! Say hi if you catch me in the mountains!
